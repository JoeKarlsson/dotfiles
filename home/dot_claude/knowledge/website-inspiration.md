# Website inspiration: Joe's saved sites

Joe's backlog of sites he likes, for the research step of [building a site](/building-a-site.md). Take ideas, never a site's look: the direction still comes from the goal and [the web design rules](/web-design-no-ai-slop.md). The notes are from desktop screenshots, so motion is described where it showed up; open the live site before borrowing a motion idea. Add new saves here with the date, his words in quotes where he gave any.

## What the set has in common (2026-11-24)

1. **Type as the picture.** A wordmark or headline fills the screen and is the hero: LAM, MCNK, FÁCIL, Virgil, USELESS, BOOOK.LAND, Safari Riot's outlined titles. Four of the saves are type foundries.
2. **The list is the page.** Indexes set big carry the design: film titles (Safari Riot), news rows (GASS), courses (Carrière scène), a project table (Natasja Bökkerink), thousands of object names (Virgil Abloh), stacked edition bands (Frontier Imaginaries).
3. **One loud color per site on white, off-white or black,** often as flat fields: red, electric blue, neon green, hot pink, yellow. Strong pairs: red and dark green (GASS), hot pink and green hairlines (GT Mechanik), yellow and soft pink (Rhythm Dialogues), coral and ultramarine (Useless).
4. **Huge next to tiny.** Giant display type beside small mono or serif metadata, with almost nothing in between.
5. **Hand-drawn marks over polished media.** Scribbled frames and lettering on top of slick video (Piotr Samborski), cut-out illustrations (Beci Orpin), napkin drawings (FAN), a signature logo (Love and Money), photo collage (Kaskad).
6. **Video and photos as layered material.** Clips overlap type, each other and color fields instead of sitting in a grid (Fácil, Oh Ira, Rhythm Dialogues, Dazzle, Sing Sing).
7. **Technical-drawing language.** Hairlines, leader labels, rulers, glyph grids, exploded diagrams and dot-matrix type (Making Software, GT Mechanik, TYPE BY Arnhem, Essential).
8. **Toys and hidden play.** Something to find or poke: pet rocks with a hi-score, a falling bottle, doodles swapping into a wordmark, cards with shifting gravity, a right-click menu, live drawing, a sparkle cursor.
9. **Navigation that isn't a top bar.** Book spines, four corners, side panels, split browser windows, a sidebar menu, horizontal scrolling.

## Joe's favorites, with his notes

- [piotrsamborski.com](https://piotrsamborski.com/) (its HTTPS certificate is for another name, so browsers warn first): "Both the digital scribbles and the weird framing and the playfulness. Love the video, the color, the graphical elements, the weirdness, the non-traditional design elements." A scribbled orange "0%" loader, then a full-bleed video with the name and nav hand-lettered in orange marker over it and a "TURN ME ON" sound sticker. Work thumbnails float at odd sizes and positions in wobbly hand-drawn orange frames with warped crop edges, next to kid-like drawings. Closest match to Joe's own Cy Twombly scrawl taste.
- [essential.com](https://www.essential.com/): "love this header." Dot-matrix display type (ndot) for the headline over a faint field of numbers shaped like a map, mono body text, a playground link. The hero video is a Vimeo embed.
- [virgilabloh.com/codes](https://www.virgilabloh.com/codes): "Love how playful this site is." Thousands of object names in tiny mono columns fill the page like a catalog; a giant translucent blue "Virgil" sits over it, and a sticker-like vehicle with a "GET TICKETS" banner flies along a blue line. Layers of list, type and object on one plane.
- [ohira.design](https://ohira.design/): "Love the editorial layout of this, the video elements, and the color. Feels very sophisticated and playful." Gray ground, condensed caps, tiny blue mono nav set in columns, ".DESIGN" anchored huge in the corner, photo and video cards overlapping off-grid, an orange section where client reviews are styled as ID badges with barcodes.
- [makingsoftware.com](https://www.makingsoftware.com/): "I love the motion elements on this page ... Especially like technical diagrams and animations." Blue-line isometric diagrams (an exploded floppy disk with leader labels, a touch-screen grid, a Gaussian curve), a pixel-mono title, serif body with a drop cap, and a table of contents with dotted leaders and word counts.
- [facilagencia.com](https://facilagencia.com/): "I love the scroll motion on the page, the editorial fonts, the overlays, and the flow." A giant black FÁCIL® across the screen with project videos sliding over and behind the letters, lime highlighter labels stuck on each ("FÁCIL para Popeyes"), small Times text.
- [rhythmdialogues.ca](https://rhythmdialogues.ca/): "I love the soft colors, the color fields, the blocking, and the photo and video direction. I also love the menu on the sidebar and on the about page, how the page scrolls left and right." Dark, glittering full-bleed video, a staircase of yellow quote cards (Beckett), a yellow sidebar menu, soft pink and cyanotype-blue fields, giant dotted-outline type ("Imagination is a place"), a wordmark with its letters scattered.
- [gt-mechanik.com](https://gt-mechanik.com/): "Love the colors the minimal thin lines and the playful elements." Hot pink MCNK filling the top, thin green line diagrams (cones, globes), yellow circles with letters, rulers and tick marks, huge overlapping numerals in pink and green as you scroll.

## Where this pulls against the rules

Several favorites use things [the web design rules](/web-design-no-ai-slop.md) ban: scroll-driven motion (Fácil, the horizontal scroll on Rhythm Dialogues), mono text everywhere (Bastarda, Virgil Abloh, Oh Ira), gradients (Kaskad) and pill buttons. Scroll-driven motion like Fácil's and Rhythm Dialogues' is now allowed on concept sites (Joe, 2026-11-24; the rules' exception section says how). For the rest, offer the idea and flag the rule it bends.
